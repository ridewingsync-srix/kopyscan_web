Privacy Policy

KopyScan is designed around practical, local document workflows. This policy explains the permissions, files, and third-party advertising involved.

Effective date: 9 October 2026

IntroductionCamera PermissionLocal FilesStorage AccessAdvertisingThird-Party ServicesData RetentionChildrenChangesContact

1. Introduction

This policy applies to the KopyScan Android application. KopyScan provides document scanning, OCR, PDF creation, local document management, and file conversion tools. No account is required for the current local scanning workflow.

2. Camera Permission

KopyScan may request camera permission so you can capture documents. Camera access is used for document scanning. KopyScan does not require microphone, location, contacts, SMS, or other unrelated permissions for this purpose.

3. Local Files and Documents

Scanned documents, processed page images, OCR results, and generated files are stored locally in app-controlled storage unless you choose to save or share them elsewhere. Core scan processing is designed to run locally on the device where supported.

Files you choose through Android's system picker remain under your control. KopyScan does not provide cloud sync or account-based document storage.

4. Storage Access

KopyScan does not require broad storage permission for normal operation. When you select source files or save a PDF to a user-visible location, Android's file or document picker lets you choose the file and destination. The operating system grants access to the specific items you select.

5. Advertising

The free version of KopyScan may display banner advertisements provided through Google Mobile Ads / AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may process device or advertising-related information according to their policies and applicable consent requirements. Depending on settings and jurisdiction, this may include IP-derived approximate location, app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or advertising identifiers.

KopyScan uses Google's supported consent mechanism where required. Advertising does not change the local storage model for your scanned document contents.

6. Third-Party Services

KopyScan integrates Google Mobile Ads / AdMob for advertising. Google's handling of information is governed by its own privacy policies and controls. KopyScan does not currently claim integration with separate analytics, cloud storage, or account-sync services.

7. Data Retention

Locally stored scans and documents remain on your device until you delete them, remove the app and its data, or otherwise manage them through Android. Files exported to a user-selected destination or shared to another application are controlled by that destination or recipient.

8. Children

KopyScan is a general document utility and is not directed specifically to children. Advertising and consent settings should be configured in accordance with the intended audience and applicable law before public release.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when KopyScan's features, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material revisions are published.
